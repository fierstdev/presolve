# Benchmark methodology

Presolve treats committed canonical products and browser behavior as correctness
evidence, not elapsed-time measurements. The [reproducibility lanes](../../docs/reproducibility-lanes.md)
reserve host observations as non-gating, while the [Phase K benchmark corpus](../../fixtures/phase-k-benchmarks/README.md)
records committed workload inputs and methodology.

This page intentionally reports no comparative result, machine ranking, or
performance promise. Any future observation remains separate from correctness
and cannot change a pass/fail outcome.
