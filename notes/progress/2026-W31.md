implemented the first canonical slice. `defineForm` and its typed `field`
authoring surface are now public. V2 authority request/response schema v5
classifies `defineForm` by resolved TypeScript identity, lowers it into the
canonical authored model, and projects it into the existing Form entity.

was added. Field schema, validation, submission, bindings, and cold/resume

public TypeScript checks passed. Beta readiness moved from 76% to 79%.

Focused parser, canonical-lowering, V2 Form ASM, TypeScript-authority, CLI

to 82%.
